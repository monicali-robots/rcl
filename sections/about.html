About

The Robot Contact Lab (RCL) is in the Mechanical Engineering Department at the University of Washington. Our group studies the physical contact interface between robot and world, to design robots capable of real-world dexterous manipulation. Our work focuses on physical experiments and hardware design to explore these questions:

  • How do soft contacts deform and how is that relevant to grasping?
  • How can we improve grasping in unideal contact conditions, such as underwater?
  • What tactile information is useful?
  • How can we design tactile sensors that are robust and deployable?

And, we look forward to building cool robots along the way.
